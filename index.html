About Our Agency

A dedicated team of professionals committed to making every author's voice heard across the globe through innovative marketing.

We Believe Every Author Deserves Visibility

Since our founding, Author Visibility Agency has been at the forefront of author marketing. We combine deep industry knowledge with cutting edge digital strategies to create campaigns that not only increase visibility but build lasting reader communities around every author we serve. Our commitment to authenticity and measurable results sets us apart in the industry.

Our Story

Author Visibility Agency was born from a simple yet powerful belief: that every author, regardless of genre or experience level, deserves the opportunity to reach their ideal readers. Our founders, who spent years in the publishing industry, recognized a critical gap between great writing and effective marketing.

We set out to bridge that gap by building a team of passionate professionals who understand both the art of storytelling and the science of marketing. Today, we are proud to have helped over 200 authors across 30 countries grow their readership and achieve their publishing goals.

Our approach is deeply personal. We take the time to understand each author's unique voice, vision, and objectives before crafting a customized strategy. This ensures that every campaign we create is authentic, targeted, and designed for maximum impact in the real world.

Our Philosophy

We believe that great books deserve great marketing. But more than that, we believe in building genuine connections between authors and readers. Our marketing strategies are never about gimmicks or empty promises; they are about creating sustainable visibility that grows over time.

Every member of our team is deeply committed to the success of the authors we serve. We celebrate your wins as our own and work relentlessly to overcome challenges together. This collaborative spirit is at the heart of everything we do at Author Visibility Agency.

We stay ahead of industry trends, continuously refine our strategies, and invest in the latest tools and technologies to ensure our authors always have a competitive edge in the ever evolving publishing marketplace.
