Who We Are

About Our Agency

We are a passionate team of marketers, strategists, and book lovers committed to helping authors achieve the visibility they deserve.

Empowering Authors Since 2019

Author Visibility Agency was founded with a singular mission: to bridge the gap between talented authors and the readers who need their stories. In an increasingly crowded publishing landscape, we provide the strategic expertise and creative firepower to make your book stand out.

From debut novelists to established bestsellers, our tailored marketing campaigns have helped over 200 authors across 30+ countries amplify their reach, grow their readership, and achieve lasting success in the literary world.

Our approach combines data-driven advertising, authentic reader engagement, media relations, and creative storytelling to build comprehensive visibility strategies that deliver measurable results.
