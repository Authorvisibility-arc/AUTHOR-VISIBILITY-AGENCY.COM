Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started with us.

What genres do you work with?

We work with all genres — romance, thriller, fantasy, sci-fi, non-fiction, memoir, self-help, children's books, and more. Our strategies are fully customised to your specific genre and target reader demographic.

How soon will I see results?

Most authors see measurable results within 30-60 days. Social media growth and email list building start almost immediately, while book sales typically improve significantly within 60-90 days of campaign launch.

Do you work with self-published and traditionally published authors?

Absolutely! We work with both self-published and traditionally published authors. Whether you're indie or with a major publisher, our strategies are tailored to maximise your visibility and sales.

What makes Author Visibility Agency different?

Led by Carine Levi, we're authors and readers ourselves. We combine data-driven strategies with genuine passion for books and storytelling, understanding the publishing industry from the inside out.

How do I get started?

Simply email us at carine@authorvisibilityagency.com. We'll review your author platform, discuss your goals, and outline a personalised growth plan — free with absolutely no obligation.

What book promotion services are included?

We offer full-spectrum promotion: book launches, author branding, social media, email marketing, paid advertising, community building, PR & media outreach, book SEO & metadata, cover design, book trailers, review generation, and international distribution.
